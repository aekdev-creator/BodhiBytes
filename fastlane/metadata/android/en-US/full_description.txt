Discover peace and wisdom throughout your day with BodhiBytes. Explore an engaging collection of bite-sized lessons, traditional Zen koans, and foundational teachings from masters like Santideva. Designed for both beginners and experienced practitioners, BodhiBytes helps you cultivate mindfulness, reflection, and inner calm in just a few minutes a day.

Features:
- Daily bite-sized lessons on Buddhist philosophy and practice.
- A curated repository of Zen koans for contemplation.
- Wisdom and verses from Santideva's teachings.
- Track your learning journey and progress seamlessly.
- Clean, distraction-free open-source design.
